Teaching Strategies
Final Exam

●   The final exam is a 3-hour invigilated exam in person at CSE
    labs during exam period at the end of the term.
●   The exam runs in a closed environment (no internet) but with
    relevant documentation provided, with no AI assistance allowed.
●   The exam will be based on the content of the course, including
    lectures, tutorials, labs, and the major project.
●   A mark of at least 40% on the final exam is required to pass the
    course.
